#INDICATOR_NAME
"BB_Sockeye_Inshore_Run_Size"

#DESCRIPTION
"Annual inshore run size of Bristol Bay sockeye salmon (in millions of fish), calculated as the sum of catches in five terminal fishing districts, plus the escapement of sockeye to nine major river systems. The 2023-2025 run size estimate are preliminary and subject to change until fish ticket numbers are finalized. Proposed sign of the relationship is negative and the time series is lagged five years for intermediate stage indicator analysis."

#STATUS_TRENDS
"The preliminary 2026 estimate of 45.2 million sockeye salmon is a slight decrease from 2025, but still above the long-term mean."

#FACTORS
"Increases in sockeye salmon abundance in the Bering Sea have been associated with increased prey abundance and a nonlinear relationship with sea surface temperature (Yasumiishi et al., 2024)."

#IMPLICATIONS
"The recent high production stanza for sockeye salmon and record high run sizes observed in 2021-2022 suggest the potential for increased predation on larval red king crab in Bristol Bay."

#REFERENCES
"Yasumiishi, E. M., Cunningham, C. J., Farley Jr., E. V., Eisner, L. B., Strasburger, W. W., Dimond, J. A., and Irvin, P. 2024. Biological and environmental covariates of juvenile sockeye salmon distribution and abundance in the southeastern Bering Sea, 2002-2018. Ecology and Evolution, 14: e11195."
